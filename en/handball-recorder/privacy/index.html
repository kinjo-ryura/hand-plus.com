Privacy Policy

Applies to: Handball Log (HandballRecorder) / the website hand-plus.com
Last updated: 8 November 2026

Handball Log (“the App”) and the website hand-plus.com run by the developer (“the Site”, including its demo pages) respect your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled in the App and on the Site.

The App and the Site are handled differently. Only the Site includes an analytics tool; the App does not. Each section states which one it is about.

1. Information we collect

The developer does not collect your personal information. All data you enter in the App (match events, team information, player information, player photos, videos on your device and so on) is stored only on your own device and is never sent to the developer’s servers (the developer does not run any servers).

However, if you use embedded YouTube playback in the App, Google (YouTube), a third party, may obtain information about your viewing. See “3. Sharing with and collection by third parties” and “4. Analytics, advertising and tracking” for details.

About the Site. To understand how the Site is viewed, we use an analytics service (Cloudflare Web Analytics). It collects statistics such as the path of the page viewed, the referrer, the browser type, the country and page performance, and does not collect information that identifies you. See “4. Analytics, advertising and tracking” for details.

2. Network connections

The App connects to the following external services. None of these connections send the data you have entered in the App.

  • GitHub: downloading the match, highlight and competition data shown in ‘Featured matches’, and team roster data, from public repositories (download only)
  • YouTube: embedded playback of the video URLs you enter (YouTube IFrame Player). See the next section for the data Google collects during embedded playback
  • Apple (App Store): checking whether a new version of the App is available. The App queries public App Store information (the iTunes Search API) with the App’s identifier and a country (download only)

Use of YouTube is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

In addition, only when you use ‘Sync with another device’, the App connects directly to a nearby device.

  • The App connects only to the device you choose on the sync screen and connect to with a 6-digit code. The connection goes over the same Wi-Fi network or a direct connection between the devices, and does not pass through the developer’s servers or any third-party service
  • What is sent and received is your match events and team and player information (player photos are not sent). The connection is encrypted using the 6-digit code as the key
  • The first time you open the sync screen, iOS asks for permission to use the local network. If you do not allow it, everything other than sync still works

In addition to the above, the Site connects to the following.

  • Cloudflare: analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics). When you open a page, information about the page viewed and the referrer is sent

3. Sharing with and collection by third parties

The developer does not share or sell your data to third parties (the developer does not hold your data).

On the other hand, embedded YouTube playback in the App uses a third-party service provided by Google (YouTube IFrame Player). When an embedded video is played, Google may collect viewing data such as playback actions and information about the adverts shown, and may link it with cookies, your IP address, your Google account and so on. This data is handled under Google’s privacy policy, and the developer cannot access it. For who is responsible for the collection and transmission to Google, and for your rights, see “8. Controller, legal basis and your rights”.

The Site’s analytics is a third-party service provided by Cloudflare, Inc. Because of how the connection works, your IP address is sent to Cloudflare, but Cloudflare states that it does not use IP addresses or User Agents to identify individuals (fingerprinting). It is handled under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The App does not include any analytics tools or advertising SDKs. The developer does not measure how you use the App.

However, the embedded YouTube player may show adverts during playback, and Google may use viewing data to personalise adverts and measure their effectiveness (tracking). For this reason, the App’s privacy label on the App Store declares “Data Used to Track You” (product interaction and advertising data).

Before you play an embedded video for the first time, the App explains on its own screen what information is sent to Google and why, and asks for your consent. Only if you agree does it then ask for permission to track through the iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialogue. No connection is made to YouTube until you have both consented and allowed tracking. If you do not consent or do not allow tracking, embedded playback is unavailable, and no connection is made to YouTube. You can withdraw your consent at any time under “YouTube playback” in the App’s Settings tab, and your permission to track in the iOS Settings app. When you withdraw your consent, the App also deletes the YouTube cookies and similar data it has stored on your device. Features that do not use YouTube, such as recording and totalling matches and playing videos on your device, work whether or not you consent or allow tracking.

The in-app consent screen is in version 1.7.0 and later. In versions up to 1.6.6, permission given in the ATT dialogue is treated as consent.

The Site includes Cloudflare Web Analytics. It is used to understand how often each page is viewed, and has the following properties.

  • It does not use cookies (nor does it store anything on your device, such as in localStorage). For this reason, we do not show a cookie consent banner
  • It does not fingerprint you to identify individuals. A “visit” is counted by whether the referrer is another site, and viewers are not tracked or identified individually
  • The part of the URL after “?” (the query), after “#” and any credentials are removed before sending. Only the path of the page is recorded
  • The developer only looks at the statistics as view counts per page, and does not use them to personalise adverts

5. Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Deleting your data

All data you record in the App is stored on your device. Deleting the App deletes all related data from your device.

8. Controller, legal basis and your rights

This section gives the information that the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws in the EU, the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland require for embedded YouTube playback in the App.

  • Controller: Kinjo Ryura (an individual developer in Japan). Contact: handball.analysis.app@gmail.com
  • Information and purposes: The developer does not process your personal data. When the embedded player loads, information such as your device’s IP address, cookies and how you interact with videos is sent to Google, which uses it to deliver videos, show and personalise adverts, and analyse usage (sections 3 and 4)
  • Legal basis: Your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time (section 4). Withdrawing it does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal
  • Relationship with Google: For the collection of information when the embedded player loads and its transmission to Google, the developer and Google (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and Switzerland, Google LLC elsewhere) may be joint controllers (Article 26 GDPR). The developer is responsible for explaining and obtaining your consent before the transmission and for providing the information in this section. Google is the controller for processing after the transmission, under the Google Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive or have access to the transmitted information. You can exercise your rights against either the developer or Google
  • Retention and transfers outside the EU: The developer does not store the information. How long Google keeps it, and the safeguards for transfers outside the EU (such as to the United States), are set out in the Google Privacy Policy
  • Providing the information is optional: If you do not consent, features that do not use YouTube still work. The developer does not carry out automated decision-making, including profiling
  • Your rights: You have the right to request access to, rectification or erasure of your data, restriction of processing, to object, and to data portability. As the developer holds no data, you can also make requests about data held by Google through your Google Account settings or the contact points in the Google Privacy Policy
  • Complaints: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (data protection authority), in particular in the country where you live, where you work or where you believe an infringement took place
