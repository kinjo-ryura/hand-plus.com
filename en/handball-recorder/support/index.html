App: Handball Log (HandballRecorder)

Handball Log is an iPhone and iPad app for recording handball matches while you watch the video, then seeing the stats, the goal difference over time and the highlights after the match, all on one device.

Getting started

  1. Choose a match in ‘Featured matches’ on the Matches tab to see its summary and highlights without recording anything
  2. To record your own match, first add your teams on the Teams tab, then create a match with the + button on the Matches tab. Choose a mode: ‘Video’ (record while watching a YouTube video or a video on your device), ‘Timer’ (record against the match clock, without a video) or ‘Highlights’ (record only the moments you want from a video)
  3. On the recording screen, tap ‘Goal’, ‘Missed shot’ and so on, then choose the player. In video mode, the position in the video at that moment is saved too
  4. Open ‘Summary’ from the match details to see stats by team, by player and by half, and the goal difference over time. Use ‘Share’ at the top right of the summary to send a stats card (image) or a match report (PDF)

Frequently asked questions

I want to try it without recording

Straight after installing, just choose a match in ‘Featured matches’ on the Matches tab to try the summary and highlights. There is also a demo you can try in your browser without installing the app (in Japanese).

I started a match with the timer and want to link a video later

Open the ‘…’ menu on the match details screen and choose ‘Convert to video mode...’.

I want to keep my matches the same on another iPhone or iPad

Choose ‘Sync with another device’ from the ‘…’ menu at the top right of the Matches tab. Open the sync screen on both devices and connect with the 6-digit code; you can check what will change on each device before saving. For a device that isn’t nearby, use ‘Send all matches’ to put matches into one file and send it by Messages or similar.

I’d like my recorded match added to Featured matches

Please contact us on X (Twitter).
